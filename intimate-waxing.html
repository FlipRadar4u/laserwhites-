The treatment

About Intimate Waxing

Intimate waxing removes hair from the root across the bikini area, with three levels depending on how much you want taken. A bikini wax tidies the edges so nothing shows outside underwear or a swimming costume. A French wax leaves a narrow strip at the front. A Hollywood removes everything.

It is the treatment people are most nervous about booking, and the thing worth knowing is that Laser Whites is a single-therapist salon in a private treatment room. There is no reception, no waiting area and nobody else about. Daisy does this several times a week.

Regrowth takes around five weeks, and repeated waxing tends to make it finer over time.

What happens at your appointment

  1. You are shown into the private treatment room and left to get ready.
  2. You agree exactly how much is coming off before anything starts.
  3. Wax is applied and removed in small sections, working steadily.
  4. Strays are tidied and the skin is soothed.

Preparing for your visit,
and looking after the results

Before your appointment

  • Arrive with clean skin where possible.
  • Avoid alcohol for 24 hours before injectable treatments.
  • Avoid taking aspirin or ibuprofen for 24–48 hours beforehand unless prescribed by your GP.
  • Stay well hydrated.
  • Let us know about any medical conditions, medications or recent procedures.
  • Please arrive on time, as late arrivals may need to be rescheduled.

Patch tests are not carried out. If you have ever reacted to a tint, a wax or a skincare product, please tell us before booking.

Aftercare

Daisy will talk you through aftercare for this treatment before you leave, and you are welcome to ask beforehand if you would like to know what is involved first.

Who is not suitable for treatment

Some treatments may not be suitable if you:

  • Are pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • Have an active skin infection, cold sore or open wound in the treatment area.
  • Have certain medical conditions.
  • Are taking medication that may affect healing or increase bleeding.
  • Have recently had surgery in the treatment area.
  • Have severe allergies to treatment ingredients.

For injectable treatments, such as anti-wrinkle injections and fat dissolving, a full medical consultation will always be carried out before treatment. If you are unsure whether you are suitable, please get in touch before booking.

FAQs

Intimate Waxing,
your questions answered

How much is a Hollywood wax in Wirral?

£50 at Laser Whites in Eastham for a full hour. A French wax is £35 and a bikini wax £25, both 45 minutes.

What is the difference between a bikini, French and Hollywood wax?

A bikini wax tidies the edges only. A French wax leaves a narrow strip at the front and removes the rest. A Hollywood removes everything.

Is it embarrassing?

Laser Whites is a private treatment room with a single therapist and no waiting area, so there is nobody else about. Tell Daisy it is your first time and she will talk you through what is happening as she goes.

How long does intimate waxing last?

Around five weeks. Waxing removes the hair from the root, and with repeat visits regrowth tends to come back finer.

Should I shave beforehand?

No. The hair needs to be long enough for the wax to grip. Leave it and check with Daisy when you book if you are unsure.
