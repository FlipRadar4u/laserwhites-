The treatment

About Anti-Wrinkle Injections

Anti-wrinkle injections relax the small muscles that crease the skin every time you frown, raise your eyebrows or smile. With those muscles quietened, the skin above them stops folding along the same lines, and the existing lines soften as they are no longer being reinforced dozens of times a day.

The three areas usually treated are the forehead, the frown lines between the brows, and the crow's feet at the outer corners of the eyes. You can have one, two or three treated, and the price steps with the number rather than being charged per area separately.

It works best on lines caused by movement. Lines that sit there when your face is completely still are a different problem and need a different answer, which Daisy will tell you honestly rather than sell you the wrong treatment.

What happens at your appointment

  1. A consultation covers your medical history, what you want to change, and what is realistic.
  2. You are asked to make several expressions so the muscles being treated can be mapped on your face.
  3. The injections are given. It is a small number of injections with a very fine needle and takes a few minutes.
  4. You are given aftercare instructions before you leave.
  5. Results appear gradually over the following days rather than immediately.

Before and after

Preparing for your visit,
and looking after the results

Before your appointment

  • Arrive with clean skin where possible.
  • Avoid alcohol for 24 hours before injectable treatments.
  • Avoid taking aspirin or ibuprofen for 24–48 hours beforehand unless prescribed by your GP.
  • Stay well hydrated.
  • Let us know about any medical conditions, medications or recent procedures.
  • Please arrive on time, as late arrivals may need to be rescheduled.

Aftercare

For the first 24 hours:

  • Stay upright for at least 4 hours.
  • Avoid strenuous exercise.
  • Do not rub or massage the treated area.
  • Avoid alcohol on the day of treatment.
  • Avoid facials and excessive heat for 24 hours.

Consultation

A consultation is required before treatment. This includes a medical assessment and prescription with our prescribing practitioner, as anti-wrinkle injections are a prescription-only medicine in the UK.

Who is not suitable for treatment

Some treatments may not be suitable if you:

  • Are pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • Have an active skin infection, cold sore or open wound in the treatment area.
  • Have certain medical conditions.
  • Are taking medication that may affect healing or increase bleeding.
  • Have recently had surgery in the treatment area.
  • Have severe allergies to treatment ingredients.

For injectable treatments, such as anti-wrinkle injections and fat dissolving, a full medical consultation will always be carried out before treatment. If you are unsure whether you are suitable, please get in touch before booking.

FAQs

Anti-Wrinkle Injections,
your questions answered

How much are anti-wrinkle injections on the Wirral?

£90 for one area at Laser Whites in Eastham, £120 for two areas and £150 for all three.

How long do anti-wrinkle injections last?

Around four months, which is the interval we rebook at. It varies between people and tends to lengthen slightly with repeat treatments.

Which areas can be treated?

The three usual upper face areas: the forehead, the frown lines between the brows, and the crow's feet at the outer corners of the eyes.

How long does the appointment take?

The injections themselves take a few minutes. We block out twenty minutes, and a consultation happens before treatment.

Will I look frozen?

That comes from overtreatment. Say what you want at your consultation, and if you want to keep movement, say so clearly.

When will I see results?

Not immediately. Anti-wrinkle injections take a few days to begin working and settle over roughly two weeks.
