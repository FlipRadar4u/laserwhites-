The treatment

About Skin Boosters

Skin boosters are injectables that hydrate and improve the skin from within. Unlike filler, they are not placed to add volume or change a shape. They are distributed through the skin to improve its quality: hydration, suppleness, fine lines and glow.

Four are offered, and they are genuinely different products rather than sizes of the same thing. Hyaron at £40 is the entry point, a hyaluronic acid injection that deeply hydrates and plumps. Lumi Pro at £60 goes further on skin quality. Vitaran at £90 is a polynucleotide that works on regeneration rather than hydration alone. Seventy Hyal at £100 is a high molecular weight hyaluronic acid for deep, long-lasting hydration.

Results build over the weeks after treatment and hold for around four months. A course is common, particularly for the first round.

What happens at your appointment

  1. A consultation covers your skin, your medical history, and which product suits what you want.
  2. The area is cleaned and marked out.
  3. A series of small injections places the product evenly across the area.
  4. Aftercare is explained. Small raised bumps settle over the first day or so.

Preparing for your visit,
and looking after the results

Before your appointment

  • Arrive with clean skin where possible.
  • Avoid alcohol for 24 hours before injectable treatments.
  • Avoid taking aspirin or ibuprofen for 24–48 hours beforehand unless prescribed by your GP.
  • Stay well hydrated.
  • Let us know about any medical conditions, medications or recent procedures.
  • Please arrive on time, as late arrivals may need to be rescheduled.

Aftercare

Daisy will talk you through aftercare for this treatment before you leave, and you are welcome to ask beforehand if you would like to know what is involved first.

Who is not suitable for treatment

Some treatments may not be suitable if you:

  • Are pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • Have an active skin infection, cold sore or open wound in the treatment area.
  • Have certain medical conditions.
  • Are taking medication that may affect healing or increase bleeding.
  • Have recently had surgery in the treatment area.
  • Have severe allergies to treatment ingredients.

For injectable treatments, such as anti-wrinkle injections and fat dissolving, a full medical consultation will always be carried out before treatment. If you are unsure whether you are suitable, please get in touch before booking.

FAQs

Skin Boosters,
your questions answered

What is the difference between skin boosters and filler?

Filler is placed to add volume and change a shape. Skin boosters are spread through the skin to improve its hydration and quality. They do not change your features.

How much are skin boosters in Wirral?

From £40 at Laser Whites in Eastham for Hyaron, £60 for Lumi Pro, £90 for Vitaran and £100 for Seventy Hyal.

Which skin booster should I have?

They target different things, so it is a consultation question rather than a price question. Tell Daisy what is bothering you and she will recommend one.

How long do skin boosters last?

Around four months, which is the interval we rebook at. The first course often needs more than one session.

Do they hurt?

They are injected, so there is some discomfort, and small raised bumps are normal for the first day. Ask Daisy what to expect from the specific product at your consultation.
