The treatment

About Glass Facial

The glass facial takes its name from the finish it aims at: skin so smooth and evenly hydrated that it reflects light like glass. It is a multi-step treatment rather than a single technique, layering cleansing, exfoliation, hydration and finishing steps across a full hour.

It is the most expensive facial on the menu at £50 and the one clients book before weddings, holidays and anything photographed. One of the reviews on the Google profile picks it out by name as the treatment she keeps coming back for.

What happens at your appointment

  1. Skin is thoroughly cleansed and assessed.
  2. Exfoliation clears the dull surface layer so everything after it can work.
  3. Layers of hydrating and brightening products are worked in.
  4. The treatment finishes with the steps that create the reflective, glass-like surface.

Before and after

Preparing for your visit,
and looking after the results

Before your appointment

  • Arrive with clean skin where possible.
  • Avoid alcohol for 24 hours before injectable treatments.
  • Avoid taking aspirin or ibuprofen for 24–48 hours beforehand unless prescribed by your GP.
  • Stay well hydrated.
  • Let us know about any medical conditions, medications or recent procedures.
  • Please arrive on time, as late arrivals may need to be rescheduled.

Aftercare

For the first 24 hours:

  • Wear SPF daily.
  • Avoid exfoliating products.
  • Avoid saunas, steam rooms and intense exercise.
  • Keep skin well hydrated.

Who is not suitable for treatment

Some treatments may not be suitable if you:

  • Are pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • Have an active skin infection, cold sore or open wound in the treatment area.
  • Have certain medical conditions.
  • Are taking medication that may affect healing or increase bleeding.
  • Have recently had surgery in the treatment area.
  • Have severe allergies to treatment ingredients.

For injectable treatments, such as anti-wrinkle injections and fat dissolving, a full medical consultation will always be carried out before treatment. If you are unsure whether you are suitable, please get in touch before booking.

FAQs

Glass Facial,
your questions answered

What is a glass facial?

A multi-step facial aimed at a very smooth, evenly hydrated, light-reflecting finish, the look known as glass skin. It runs a full hour and layers cleansing, exfoliation and hydration steps.

How much is a glass facial in Wirral?

£50 at Laser Whites in Eastham, for a full hour.

How long does the glow last?

Ask Daisy when you book, as it depends on your skin and your routine at home. We rebook around every five weeks.

Is it good before a wedding?

It is one of the most requested treatments for exactly that. Book it a few days beforehand rather than the same morning so the skin has settled.

Which is your best facial?

The glass facial is the most involved on the menu. Whether it is the right one for your skin depends on what you want to change, so ask Daisy rather than assuming the dearest is the best fit.
