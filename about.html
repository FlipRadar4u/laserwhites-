Meet Daisy

The face behind

Hi, I'm Daisy, the owner, therapist and sole face behind Laser Whites Beauty & Aesthetics in Eastham, Wirral.

My story

Built on passion,
skill & care

I started Laser Whites because beauty is something I genuinely love. It's never just been a job to me. It's the way I help people feel like the best version of themselves. Whether you're coming in for a quick brow tint or a full aesthetic treatment, every client gets my personal care from start to finish.

Running the salon solo means you'll always deal with me directly. No hand-offs, no rushing, no strangers. Just a relaxed, one-to-one experience in a space I've built to feel calm, clean, and truly welcoming. My clients become regulars, and I love that.

With over 10 years of experience and more than 3,000 treatments behind me, I've built a reputation on the Wirral that I'm really proud of. I'm fully qualified across beauty therapy, aesthetics, and advanced treatments, and I keep training, because my clients deserve the best I can give them. I see clients from Eastham, Bebington, Bromborough, Heswall, Birkenhead, and all across Merseyside.

Accredited CPD Provider
