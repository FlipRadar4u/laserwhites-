The treatment

About Japanese Head Spa

A Japanese head spa is a scalp treatment rather than a hair treatment, though your hair benefits from it. It combines a deep cleanse of the scalp, an extended massage, and conditioning work, carried out with you lying back over a basin with proper equipment rather than perched at a salon sink.

The scalp is skin, and it gets congested with product, oil and dead cells in exactly the way facial skin does. Clearing that is the practical half of the treatment. The other half is simply that a forty-five to ninety minute head and scalp massage is extraordinarily relaxing, which is why people book it as a treat rather than as maintenance.

Four packages are offered. The mini at twenty minutes is designed for children. The bronze, silver and gold run forty-five, sixty and ninety minutes, each adding more massage and more conditioning work.

What happens at your appointment

  1. You lie back over the basin and get comfortable. Nothing about this treatment is rushed.
  2. The scalp is cleansed thoroughly, clearing product build-up and congestion.
  3. An extended scalp, neck and shoulder massage follows. This is the longest part.
  4. Conditioning treatment is worked through the hair and scalp.
  5. Hair is rinsed and finished.

Before your appointment

  • Arrive with clean skin where possible.
  • Avoid alcohol for 24 hours before injectable treatments.
  • Avoid taking aspirin or ibuprofen for 24–48 hours beforehand unless prescribed by your GP.
  • Stay well hydrated.
  • Let us know about any medical conditions, medications or recent procedures.
  • Please arrive on time, as late arrivals may need to be rescheduled.

Aftercare

Daisy will talk you through aftercare for this treatment before you leave, and you are welcome to ask beforehand if you would like to know what is involved first.

Who is not suitable for treatment

Some treatments may not be suitable if you:

  • Are pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • Have an active skin infection, cold sore or open wound in the treatment area.
  • Have certain medical conditions.
  • Are taking medication that may affect healing or increase bleeding.
  • Have recently had surgery in the treatment area.
  • Have severe allergies to treatment ingredients.

For injectable treatments, such as anti-wrinkle injections and fat dissolving, a full medical consultation will always be carried out before treatment. If you are unsure whether you are suitable, please get in touch before booking.
