The treatment

About Hydro-Dermabrasion

Hydro-dermabrasion uses a pressurised water jet to lift away dead skin while simultaneously flooding the surface with hydration. It is the gentler relative of microdermabrasion: the same resurfacing effect, but wet rather than dry, which is far kinder to skin that reacts badly to abrasion.

Because it hydrates as it exfoliates, the immediate result is plumper and dewier than a dry resurfacing treatment. It is the one to choose if your skin is dry, dehydrated or on the sensitive side.

At a full hour it is the longest facial on the menu apart from the glass facial and the bespoke option.

What happens at your appointment

  1. Your skin is cleansed and assessed.
  2. The handpiece passes over the face delivering the water jet, exfoliating and hydrating in one motion.
  3. Loosened debris is drawn away as she works.
  4. Hydrating products are applied to finish.

Before and after

Preparing for your visit,
and looking after the results

Before your appointment

  • Arrive with clean skin where possible.
  • Avoid alcohol for 24 hours before injectable treatments.
  • Avoid taking aspirin or ibuprofen for 24–48 hours beforehand unless prescribed by your GP.
  • Stay well hydrated.
  • Let us know about any medical conditions, medications or recent procedures.
  • Please arrive on time, as late arrivals may need to be rescheduled.

Aftercare

For the first 24 hours:

  • Wear SPF daily.
  • Avoid exfoliating products.
  • Avoid saunas, steam rooms and intense exercise.
  • Keep skin well hydrated.

Who is not suitable for treatment

Some treatments may not be suitable if you:

  • Are pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • Have an active skin infection, cold sore or open wound in the treatment area.
  • Have certain medical conditions.
  • Are taking medication that may affect healing or increase bleeding.
  • Have recently had surgery in the treatment area.
  • Have severe allergies to treatment ingredients.

For injectable treatments, such as anti-wrinkle injections and fat dissolving, a full medical consultation will always be carried out before treatment. If you are unsure whether you are suitable, please get in touch before booking.

What is hydro-dermabrasion?

Exfoliation using a pressurised water jet rather than an abrasive tip, which removes dead skin and delivers hydration in the same pass.

Is it suitable for sensitive skin?

It is generally the kinder of the two resurfacing options because nothing abrasive touches the skin. Tell Daisy how your skin normally reacts and she will advise.

How often should I have it?

Around every four weeks. We send a reminder when you are due.

Is there downtime?

No. Skin usually looks better immediately rather than needing to settle.
