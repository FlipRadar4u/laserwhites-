The treatment

About Teeth Whitening

Teeth whitening at Laser Whites uses a whitening gel activated by an LED lamp. The gel lifts staining that has built up in the surface of the enamel from tea, coffee, red wine and smoking, and the light speeds that reaction up so the whole thing is finished inside a single appointment rather than over weeks of home trays.

It is the treatment Daisy is best known for on the Wirral. Nine of the twenty reviews on her Google profile mention it by name, and several of those clients now travel across Merseyside to have it done.

What happens at your appointment

  1. We talk through what you are hoping for and check you are happy to go ahead.
  2. Your lips and gums are shielded so the gel only touches the teeth.
  3. The whitening gel is applied across the front teeth.
  4. The LED lamp sits in front of you and you lie back for the session. You can talk, listen to music, or have your lashes or brows done at the same time.
  5. The gel is removed and you see the result there and then. There is nothing to wear home.

Before your appointment

  • Arrive with clean skin where possible.
  • Avoid alcohol for 24 hours before injectable treatments.
  • Avoid taking aspirin or ibuprofen for 24–48 hours beforehand unless prescribed by your GP.
  • Stay well hydrated.
  • Let us know about any medical conditions, medications or recent procedures.
  • Please arrive on time, as late arrivals may need to be rescheduled.

Aftercare

Daisy will talk you through aftercare for this treatment before you leave, and you are welcome to ask beforehand if you would like to know what is involved first.

Who is not suitable for treatment

Some treatments may not be suitable if you:

  • Are pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • Have an active skin infection, cold sore or open wound in the treatment area.
  • Have certain medical conditions.
  • Are taking medication that may affect healing or increase bleeding.
  • Have recently had surgery in the treatment area.
  • Have severe allergies to treatment ingredients.

For injectable treatments, such as anti-wrinkle injections and fat dissolving, a full medical consultation will always be carried out before treatment. If you are unsure whether you are suitable, please get in touch before booking.

FAQs

Teeth Whitening,
your questions answered

How long does teeth whitening take?

The whitening itself takes an hour. We block out an hour and a quarter for your appointment so nothing is rushed at either end. The two people offer and the whitening with gems both run to an hour and twenty.

How much is teeth whitening on the Wirral?

£40 for one person at Laser Whites in Eastham. Two people treated in the same appointment is £70, so £35 each. Adding a teeth gem to a whitening session is £10 rather than the usual £20.

How often should I have my teeth whitened?

Most clients come back around every six months. Some come more often, particularly if they drink a lot of tea, coffee or red wine. We send a reminder when you are due rather than leave you to remember.

Can two people be whitened together?

Yes, and it works out cheaper. The two people offer is £70 for both and runs as a single eighty minute appointment, so you book one slot and bring the other person with you.

Can I have anything else done at the same time?

Yes. Because you are lying back with the lamp in front of you, Daisy can do a lash lift, lash tint or brow treatment while your teeth are whitening. It adds nothing to the length of your appointment.

Do I have to pay in full when I book?

No. A £10 deposit holds the appointment and the balance is paid on the day. Cancel with more than 48 hours notice and the deposit carries over to your next booking.
