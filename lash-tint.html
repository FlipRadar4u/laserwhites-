The treatment

About Lash Tint

A lash tint darkens your natural lashes right to the tips with a semi permanent tint. Fair lashes, and the ends of most lashes, are far lighter than people realise, and darkening them defines the eye without anything being added to it.

At £5 it is the cheapest treatment on the menu and one of the quickest. Plenty of clients have it done alongside something else rather than as a visit of its own.

What happens at your appointment

  1. Your lower lashes are shielded and your eyes stay closed throughout.
  2. The tint is applied along the upper and lower lashes.
  3. It develops for a few minutes.
  4. Everything is removed and combed through, and you are done.

Before your appointment

  • Arrive with clean skin where possible.
  • Avoid alcohol for 24 hours before injectable treatments.
  • Avoid taking aspirin or ibuprofen for 24–48 hours beforehand unless prescribed by your GP.
  • Stay well hydrated.
  • Let us know about any medical conditions, medications or recent procedures.
  • Please arrive on time, as late arrivals may need to be rescheduled.

Patch tests are not carried out. If you have ever reacted to a tint, a wax or a skincare product, please tell us before booking.

Aftercare

Daisy will talk you through aftercare for this treatment before you leave, and you are welcome to ask beforehand if you would like to know what is involved first.

Who is not suitable for treatment

Some treatments may not be suitable if you:

  • Are pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • Have an active skin infection, cold sore or open wound in the treatment area.
  • Have certain medical conditions.
  • Are taking medication that may affect healing or increase bleeding.
  • Have recently had surgery in the treatment area.
  • Have severe allergies to treatment ingredients.

For injectable treatments, such as anti-wrinkle injections and fat dissolving, a full medical consultation will always be carried out before treatment. If you are unsure whether you are suitable, please get in touch before booking.

FAQs

Lash Tint,
your questions answered

How long does a lash tint last?

Around four weeks. The tint sits on the lash rather than the skin, so it fades as those lashes shed and are replaced.

How much is a lash tint?

£5 at Laser Whites in Eastham, which is the full price rather than an add-on rate.

What is the difference between a lash tint and a lash lift?

A tint darkens your lashes. A lift also curls them upward from the root. The tint is £5 and lasts about four weeks; the lift and tint together are £30 and last about eight.

How long does it take?

Fifteen minutes, and we block out twenty for the appointment.

Do I pay when I book?

The £5 treatments are paid in full at booking rather than taking a deposit, so there is nothing to pay on the day.
