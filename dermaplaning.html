The treatment

About Dermaplaning

Dermaplaning uses a sterile surgical blade held at an angle against the skin to shave away the layer of dead cells on the surface along with the fine vellus hair, the peach fuzz, that grows on almost everyone's face.

The immediate difference is texture. Foundation stops catching on the fuzz and sits flat, and serums and moisturisers actually reach living skin instead of soaking into a layer of dead cells.

The hair grows back exactly as it was. It is vellus hair, which is fine and soft by nature, and cutting it does not change what it grows back as.

What happens at your appointment

  1. Your skin is cleansed and dried thoroughly.
  2. The skin is held taut and the blade is drawn across it in short strokes, section by section.
  3. The debris is wiped away as she works.
  4. The skin is soothed and finished. The luxury version adds further nourishing steps at this stage.

Before your appointment

  • Arrive with clean skin where possible.
  • Avoid alcohol for 24 hours before injectable treatments.
  • Avoid taking aspirin or ibuprofen for 24–48 hours beforehand unless prescribed by your GP.
  • Stay well hydrated.
  • Let us know about any medical conditions, medications or recent procedures.
  • Please arrive on time, as late arrivals may need to be rescheduled.

Aftercare

For the first 24 hours:

  • Avoid excessive heat and exercise.
  • Wear SPF daily.
  • Avoid exfoliating products and retinol for 3–5 days.

Who is not suitable for treatment

Some treatments may not be suitable if you:

  • Are pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • Have an active skin infection, cold sore or open wound in the treatment area.
  • Have certain medical conditions.
  • Are taking medication that may affect healing or increase bleeding.
  • Have recently had surgery in the treatment area.
  • Have severe allergies to treatment ingredients.

For injectable treatments, such as anti-wrinkle injections and fat dissolving, a full medical consultation will always be carried out before treatment. If you are unsure whether you are suitable, please get in touch before booking.

FAQs

Dermaplaning,
your questions answered

Does dermaplaning make hair grow back thicker?

No. The hair removed is vellus hair, which is fine and soft by nature. Cutting it does not change the follicle or what grows back from it. It returns exactly as it was.

How much is dermaplaning in Wirral?

£20 at Laser Whites in Eastham for standard dermaplaning, or £30 for the luxury version with added skincare steps.

How often can I have dermaplaning?

Around every four weeks, which lets the skin complete a full cycle in between. We send a reminder when you are due.

Does it hurt?

No. It is a blade drawn across the surface of the skin rather than into it. Most people describe it as a light scraping sensation and nothing more.

What is the difference between dermaplaning and microdermabrasion?

Both exfoliate. Dermaplaning uses a blade and also removes facial hair; microdermabrasion buffs the surface and does not. Dermaplaning is £20, microdermabrasion is £30.
