The treatment

About Skin Peel

A skin peel uses acids applied to the face to loosen the bonds holding dead cells to the surface, so that layer lifts away rather than sitting there dulling everything. It works chemically where dermaplaning and microdermabrasion work physically.

It is the better tool for pigmentation, congestion and dullness, because it acts across the whole surface evenly and can be taken deeper than a mechanical exfoliation comfortably can.

Peels are usually taken as a course rather than a one-off, building up over sessions. The six week interval we rebook at gives skin time to recover fully in between.

What happens at your appointment

  1. Your skin is cleansed and prepared.
  2. The peel solution is applied and left on for a controlled time, watched closely throughout.
  3. It is neutralised and removed.
  4. Soothing and protective products are applied to finish.

Before your appointment

  • Arrive with clean skin where possible.
  • Avoid alcohol for 24 hours before injectable treatments.
  • Avoid taking aspirin or ibuprofen for 24–48 hours beforehand unless prescribed by your GP.
  • Stay well hydrated.
  • Let us know about any medical conditions, medications or recent procedures.
  • Please arrive on time, as late arrivals may need to be rescheduled.

Patch tests are not carried out. If you have ever reacted to a tint, a wax or a skincare product, please tell us before booking.

Aftercare

Daisy will talk you through aftercare for this treatment before you leave, and you are welcome to ask beforehand if you would like to know what is involved first.

Who is not suitable for treatment

Some treatments may not be suitable if you:

  • Are pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • Have an active skin infection, cold sore or open wound in the treatment area.
  • Have certain medical conditions.
  • Are taking medication that may affect healing or increase bleeding.
  • Have recently had surgery in the treatment area.
  • Have severe allergies to treatment ingredients.

For injectable treatments, such as anti-wrinkle injections and fat dissolving, a full medical consultation will always be carried out before treatment. If you are unsure whether you are suitable, please get in touch before booking.

FAQs

Skin Peel,
your questions answered

How much is a skin peel in Wirral?

£30 at Laser Whites in Eastham for a 30 minute treatment.

How often can I have a skin peel?

We rebook around every six weeks, which gives the skin time to recover fully between sessions. Peels generally work best as a course.

Will my skin actually peel?

That depends on the strength used and on your skin. Daisy will tell you what to expect from the specific peel she is using on you before she starts.

Skin peel or micro-needling?

A peel works on the surface and is better for pigmentation, congestion and clarity. Micro-needling works deeper and is better for scarring, texture and firmness. The peel is £30, micro-needling is £40.

Is there anything I should do beforehand?

Yes, and it matters more with peels than with most treatments. Speak to Daisy before booking so she can tell you what to stop using and when.
