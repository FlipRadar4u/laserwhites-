About HIFU Vaginal Rejuvenation

HIFU stands for high intensity focused ultrasound. Energy is focused at a set depth beneath the surface, heating the tissue there enough to prompt collagen contraction and new collagen production, while the surface itself is left untouched. It is the same principle as the radio frequency facial, applied to a different part of the body.

It is used for laxity and tone, commonly after childbirth or through the menopause. It is non-surgical, requires no anaesthetic and has no recovery period.

This is understandably a treatment people hesitate to book. Laser Whites is a single-therapist salon in a private treatment room with no reception and no waiting area, and Daisy is the only person you will see.

What happens at your appointment

  1. A consultation covers your medical history and what you want to change.
  2. You are shown into the private treatment room and left to get ready.
  3. The HIFU handpiece delivers the focused ultrasound in a controlled pattern.
  4. Aftercare is explained before you leave.

Before and after

Preparing for your visit,
and looking after the results

Before your appointment

  • Arrive with clean skin where possible.
  • Avoid alcohol for 24 hours before injectable treatments.
  • Avoid taking aspirin or ibuprofen for 24–48 hours beforehand unless prescribed by your GP.
  • Stay well hydrated.
  • Let us know about any medical conditions, medications or recent procedures.
  • Please arrive on time, as late arrivals may need to be rescheduled.

Aftercare

Following your treatment:

  • Drink plenty of water to stay hydrated.
  • Maintain good intimate hygiene.
  • Avoid sexual intercourse for 48 hours after treatment.
  • Avoid hot baths, saunas, steam rooms and hot tubs for 48 hours.
  • Avoid strenuous exercise for 24–48 hours.
  • Mild sensitivity or a slight increase in vaginal discharge can be normal for a short time after treatment.
  • Results continue to improve over the following 8–12 weeks as your body naturally produces new collagen.

For best results, a course of treatments may be recommended depending on your individual concerns and desired outcome. This will be discussed during your consultation, where a personalised treatment plan will be created just for you.

Who is not suitable for treatment

Some treatments may not be suitable if you:

  • Are pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • Have an active skin infection, cold sore or open wound in the treatment area.
  • Have certain medical conditions.
  • Are taking medication that may affect healing or increase bleeding.
  • Have recently had surgery in the treatment area.
  • Have severe allergies to treatment ingredients.

For injectable treatments, such as anti-wrinkle injections and fat dissolving, a full medical consultation will always be carried out before treatment. If you are unsure whether you are suitable, please get in touch before booking.
