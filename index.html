FAQs

Your questions
answered

What treatments does Laser Whites Beauty & Aesthetics offer?

We offer a wide range of beauty and aesthetic treatments including teeth whitening, anti-wrinkle injections, skin boosters, micro-needling, Japanese Head Spa, dermaplaning, lash lift and tint, brow waxing and tinting, waxing, facials, and more. You can view our full treatment menu on our Services page.

Where is Laser Whites Beauty & Aesthetics located?

We're based on Calveley Avenue, Eastham, Wirral, CH62 9DS, a short drive from Bebington, Bromborough, Heswall, and the wider Wirral Peninsula. Free street parking is available nearby.

Is Laser Whites suitable for first-time clients?

Absolutely. We welcome clients of all experience levels. We'll talk you through everything before we begin and make sure you feel comfortable throughout your treatment.

What is a Japanese Head Spa?

A Japanese Head Spa is a deeply relaxing scalp treatment combining cleansing, massage, and nourishment to promote scalp health and hair growth. It's one of our most popular treatments. Clients often describe it as one of the most relaxing experiences they've ever had.

Does Laser Whites offer anti-wrinkle injections and aesthetic treatments?

Yes. We offer a range of aesthetic treatments including anti-wrinkle injections, skin boosters, micro-needling, and HIFU vaginal rejuvenation. All treatments are carried out professionally and with care.

How much do treatments at Laser Whites cost?

Our treatments start from as little as £10 for a brow wax and tint. Teeth whitening starts from £40, lash lift and tint is £30, and aesthetic treatments range from £40 upwards. We offer competitive prices for high-quality treatments on the Wirral.

How do I book an appointment at Laser Whites?

You can book online at any time. Choose your treatment, pick a slot that suits you, and your appointment is confirmed straight away. If you'd rather speak to someone, call 07711 253413. We're open Monday to Saturday, 8am to 7pm.

What areas does Laser Whites serve?

We're based in Eastham, Wirral, and welcome clients from across the Wirral Peninsula, including Bebington, Bromborough, Heswall, West Kirby, Birkenhead, and surrounding areas.
